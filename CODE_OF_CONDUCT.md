# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Dysphasia to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or experience level. We're building a
defensive security research tool; discussion should stay focused on that.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Backing claims with a reproducible measurement (this project's whole
  premise), and being equally willing to report a negative result
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on technical
  tradeoffs
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Requesting or contributing techniques that target real victims, bypass
  safety infrastructure, or otherwise cross from "defensive research" into
  "build me malware." See [`README.md#declined-techniques`](README.md#declined-techniques)
  for the specific categories this project has already ruled out and why.

## Scope

This applies within project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or
contacting the maintainers directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated.
